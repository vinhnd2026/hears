Emery Winslow · Floor scales

Series 708 hydrostatic floor scale

An above-grade stainless steel floor scale for washdown areas: the deck halves lift by hand on gas springs, exposing the whole understructure for cleaning without tools.

  • Grade 304 stainless
  • 3.75 in (about 95 mm) profile
  • No electronics under the deck

Request a quotePit-mounted 709 and 710

Why Emery Winslow hydrostatic

  • No electronics at the scale

    Hydrostatic load cells turn weight into fluid pressure. Stainless tubing carries it to a totalizer in a clean, dry area; only there does it become an electronic signal.

  • Made for water, washdown, lightning and surges

    The manufacturer lists water, high-pressure washdown, lightning, power surges, rodents and RFI/EMI among the causes of failure its load cells are designed to withstand.

  • Stainless steel construction

    Stainless steel load cells (304, with 316 available for the 136 Series) and 316 stainless tubing for corrosive and sanitary areas.

  • Made in the USA, roots in 1868

    Roots in the A.H. Emery Company, founded in 1868; joined with Winslow (founded 1896) in 1974. Built at the Seymour, Connecticut manufacturing center.

Strengths as stated by Emery Winslow Scale Company: Hydrostatic Weighing Systems brochure (06-15-2026), 120 and 136 Series sheets, and emerywinslow.com/about.

Water, condensation, chemicals, power surges

These are what shorten the life of an electronic floor scale and cause the repair and calibration issues that can stop a processing line. The Series 708 uses hydrostatic load cells with an electronics-free base and stainless steel construction for harsh washdown environments.

Because the deck lifts in place, cleaning staff never need to lift the scale, which the manufacturer notes reduces the risk of injury. The manufacturer also states that hydrostatic technology holds calibration longer.

Standard features

  • Smooth stainless deck plates, solid steel flat bar, all Grade 304
  • Gas springs to lift the deck for cleaning
  • Four stainless hydrostatic load cells and a summing totalizer in an N4X FRP enclosure
  • Type 316 stainless hardware and accessories
  • Platform described by the manufacturer for explosive atmospheres, without barriers
