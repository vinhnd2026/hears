Emery Winslow case study · Quarry · loading to exact weight

Florida Rock: loading every truck to the right weight on the first pass

A crushed stone producer that trims each load while the truck is still on the scale.

All case studies

What happened

Florida Rock Industries, one of the larger crushed stone producers in the United States, needs dependable equipment to load trucks to the exact weight ordered for short hauls and to top legal road weights for long hauls. “Everybody goes out with a full load,” says its weighmaster.

The new scales, combined with a new top-off machine, let staff trim the load to the exact amount while the truck is on the scale. Trucks no longer pull off to adjust the load and drive onto the scale a second time.

“I can add or remove material in a matter of seconds.” Philip Sanders, weighmaster.

  • Demand: every truck leaves with the right load
  • Installed: new truck scales with a top-off machine
  • Reported: no second trip onto the scale

Source: Emery Winslow Scale Company, “Pit & Quarry, Florida Rock: An Efficient Way to Weigh”, https://www.emerywinslow.com/pit-quarry-florida-rock/, accessed 15/11/2026. Read the original case study.
