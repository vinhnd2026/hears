Emery Winslow Scale · Technology

Hydrostatic™ Technology

Hydrostatic™ is Emery Winslow Scale's weighing technology: the load cells under the scale work with fluid pressure and contain no electronics. The electronics sit in a clean, dry room away from the scale.

Request a quoteEmery Winslow Scale solutions

The difference is where the electronics sit

On paper, a multi-load-cell electronic scale and a Hydrostatic™ scale look alike. The difference is the summing network. In an electronic scale, the load cells, cables and summing board sit at the scale, in the same water, dust and lightning risk as the platform.

In a Hydrostatic™ scale, the Summing Totalizer is installed in a protected place such as a control room or scale house. At the scale there are only mechanical load cells and pressure tubing. The manufacturer sums it up as: keep every environment-sensitive part in a safe location.

From load to reading

  1. 01

    The load cell turns weight into pressure

    A piston presses on a diaphragm over a thin film of fluid. Each cell has a known acting area, so pressure rises in step with the load. The manufacturer's example: 50,000 lb on 50 square inches gives 1,000 psi.

  2. 02

    Tubing carries the pressure

    Each load cell is connected to the Summing Totalizer by high-pressure copper or stainless steel tubing. The manufacturer rates the fittings to 15,000 psi.

  3. 03

    The Summing Totalizer adds the forces

    A receiver cell for each load cell turns pressure back into a vertical force. The forces are summed on one precision strain gauge transducer, which gives a millivolt signal.

  4. 04

    The indicator shows the weight

    The signal is digitised and displayed. The total does not depend on how the load is shared between the cells.

What the manufacturer states

Points below are Emery Winslow Scale's own statements. HearS confirms what applies to each project in the quotation.

  • Water and washdown

    No electronics at the scale, so water, flooding and washdown do not reach electronic parts there.

  • Lightning and surges

    No signal cable runs from the scale to the electronics, so a strike on the scale does not travel along one. Surge protection on the power supply is still recommended.

  • Corrosion and hygiene

    Load cells can be made in stainless steel or other metals chosen for the medium.

  • Shock and vibration

    The fluid damps vibration and shock loads, which suits rough duty.

  • Replacing a cell

    A cell of the same acting area can often be fitted without recalibration, according to the manufacturer.

  • Higher price, lower running cost

    The manufacturer says Hydrostatic™ costs more to buy than electronic equivalents and should be compared on cost of ownership.

Where it is used

Outdoors: tank and hopper scales, truck scales and railway track scales, where lightning, rain and flooding are the main risks.

Indoors: floor and bench scales, tank and hopper scales, mixers, monorail and crane scales, and custom systems, in plants with washdown, corrosive media, explosive dust or gas, vibration or electrical noise.

Industries named by the manufacturer include food, chemicals, transport, agriculture, fertiliser, steel and metals, and aviation.
