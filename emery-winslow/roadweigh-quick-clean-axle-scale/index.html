Why Emery Winslow hydrostatic

  • No electronics at the scale

    Hydrostatic load cells turn weight into fluid pressure. Stainless tubing carries it to a totalizer in a clean, dry area; only there does it become an electronic signal.

  • Made for water, washdown, lightning and surges

    The manufacturer lists water, high-pressure washdown, lightning, power surges, rodents and RFI/EMI among the causes of failure its load cells are designed to withstand.

  • Stainless steel construction

    Stainless steel load cells (304, with 316 available for the 136 Series) and 316 stainless tubing for corrosive and sanitary areas.

  • Made in the USA, roots in 1868

    Roots in the A.H. Emery Company, founded in 1868; joined with Winslow (founded 1896) in 1974. Built at the Seymour, Connecticut manufacturing center.

Strengths as stated by Emery Winslow Scale Company: Hydrostatic Weighing Systems brochure (06-15-2026), 120 and 136 Series sheets, and emerywinslow.com/about.

Waste, water and debris every day

In a waste transfer tunnel the pit under a scale fills with debris and liquid. With the Quick Clean scale, the crew removes the deck, power washes it, cleans the pit, unclogs the drains, replaces the deck, zeroes the scale and starts weighing again in about 30 minutes, according to the manufacturer.

The hydrostatic load cells remain in place when the weighbridge is lifted out, so the manufacturer states that recalibration is not needed. The sections are small enough to remove with a front-end loader or a small crane.

The cleaning routine described by the manufacturer

  1. 01

    Lift the deck

    Remove the weighbridge sections with a loader or small crane.

  2. 02

    Wash and clear

    Power wash the deck, clean out the pit, inspect it and unclog the drains.

  3. 03

    Replace and zero

    Set the deck back on the load cells, which never moved, and zero the scale.

  4. 04

    Weigh again

    Back in service in about 30 minutes.
