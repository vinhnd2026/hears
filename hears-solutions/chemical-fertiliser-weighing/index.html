Weighing for chemical & fertiliser plants

Vietnam consumes more than 11 million tonnes of fertiliser a year and produces around 8 million tonnes. Its largest urea plants run at world scale — Phú Mỹ at 800,000 tonnes of urea a year, Cà Mau at 800,000 tonnes design capacity with a record year above 950,000 tonnes. In these plants, tank weighing is not bookkeeping. It is process control.

What chemicals do to weighing

The medium a chemical plant measures is usually the medium that destroys the measurement.

  • Corrosion eats the measurement

    Ammonia, acids, caustic and phosphorus compounds attack electronic cells, cables and junction boxes. The first symptom is drift — the worst symptom is a wrong batch.

  • A failed cell stops the process

    Reactor and tank weighing feeds dosing and batch control. When a cell fails, production does not just lose a number; it loses control of the process.

  • Hazardous atmospheres

    Many chemical areas are classified for explosive gas or dust. A hydrostatic cell holds no electrical energy at the weighing point — one ignition source that does not need managing.

  • Spills and washdown

    Chemical spills and the washdown that follows them reach every low-mounted device. Sealed stainless cells have nothing for a spill to enter.

Process weighing that resists the process

Hydrostatic cells in stainless — with 316, titanium, Monel and Hastelloy options — weigh tanks and reactors while keeping every electronic part in the control room. The measurement survives the medium it measures, and HearS engineers the mounting, tubing and integration locally.

  • No electronics or junction boxes in the corrosive zone
  • Alloy options matched to the medium: 316, titanium, Monel, Hastelloy
  • Stable signal for dosing and batch control, integrated to PLC/SCADA
  • Retrofit to existing tanks with HearS-designed brackets

Where it fits

  • Tanks and reactors

    Process weighing for ammonia, acid, caustic and product tanks and reactors — the core control measurement.

  • Raw-material silos

    Apatite, potash, sulphur and additive silos weighed for inventory and feed control.

  • Bagging and custody scales

    High-rate bagging lines and custody truck scales at the gate — production out, by weight.

The digital layer: every weighing becomes a record

Tank and batch weighings can be recorded against the batch and the lot, so raw-material and finished-product inventory reconciles with production — and the QC record travels with the goods from receiving to finished stock.
