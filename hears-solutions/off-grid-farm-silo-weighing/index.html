Off-grid feed silo weighing for farms

Hydrostatic load cells need no power at the silo. One outdoor cabinet, run from a small solar panel and battery, shows the feed in every silo day and night and sends it to your phone.

Request a farm surveyHow it works

Status: feasibility study with ANDUOR

This is a solution packaged by HearS around hydrostatic weighing equipment. HearS is studying its feasibility with ANDUOR Energy Co., Ltd. (Công ty TNHH Năng lượng ANDUOR), our solar and battery storage (BESS) partner. It is not yet a delivered product: configuration, figures and schedule will be confirmed after the study.

Farms interested in taking part in a survey are welcome to contact us.

When you cannot see how much feed is in the silo

  • Silos run empty

    An empty silo means missed feeds, and a rush feed order usually costs extra.

  • Feed losses are hard to track

    Without weights it is hard to reconcile feed delivered, feed used and feed lost.

  • Climbing silos to check

    Someone has to climb the silo to check the level, which takes time and is unsafe.

Why wiring every silo is hard

For pig, poultry, cattle and fish farms, the silos are often far from the office and from a reliable power supply.

  • Long cable runs

    Power and network cables to each silo mean trenches, conduit and long ethernet runs between barns.

  • Power cuts

    When the farm supply drops, a mains-powered scale stops and stock readings are lost until it returns.

  • Lightning

    Tall steel silos on open land and long copper cables pick up surges that damage electronic load cells and boards.

  • Ammonia, dust and washing

    Ammonia from the barns corrodes copper and brass and speeds up corrosion of galvanised parts when damp; feed dust and washdown add to the load.

How it works

  1. 01

    Load cells with no power at the silo

    Hydrostatic load cells under the silo legs turn the weight into fluid pressure. There are no electronics or cables at the legs.

  2. 02

    Tubing to one outdoor cabinet

    Stainless tubing carries the pressure from each silo to a single cabinet nearby.

  3. 03

    Solar and a small battery run the cabinet

    Inside: the totalizer and the indicator. A small solar panel charges a LiFePO4 battery through an MPPT charge controller and supplies 12/24 V DC directly, with no inverter, day and night.

  4. 04

    Stock on your phone

    A LoRa link to a gateway at the farm office (range depends on terrain, barns and trees, and is tested at the survey) or a 4G modem sends the readings to an app or web dashboard.

Emery Winslow Scale equipment in a HearS solution

For the silo legs, HearS proposes Emery Winslow hydrostatic load cells, such as the 120 Series and 136 Series LifeMount. The manufacturer states they contain no electronics and are designed to withstand water, washdown, lightning and power surges.

The solar cabinet, battery and wireless link are engineered by HearS for each farm. They are not Emery Winslow products, and their figures are not Emery Winslow specifications.

What the farm gains

  • No power or network cables between silos and the office
  • Quick to install; more silos can be added later
  • Keeps weighing through grid outages
  • Nothing electronic at the silo for lightning, water or ammonia to damage
  • Feed stock per silo on the phone
  • Feed reordered on time, before a silo runs empty
  • Feed use reconciled per flock or batch
  • Almost no electricity cost: the cabinet runs on solar

Practical design notes

  • Cabinet

    At least IP66, in 316 stainless steel or GRP, since ammonia attacks copper and brass and speeds up corrosion of galvanising when damp. A breather vent limits condensation; connectors are corrosion resistant.

  • Heat

    A cabinet in full sun can pass 60 °C inside, so add a sunshade or double roof. LiFePO4 batteries have a charging temperature limit; the BMS stops charging when they are too hot. Put the battery in a separate shaded compartment or box, not in the hot compartment.

  • Lightning

    Silo, cabinet and solar frame are bonded to one earth. Surge protection on the solar line and the antenna. The tubing brings no wiring to the silo legs.

  • Dust

    The cabinet stands away from fill points and blowers. Any dust zone rules for the site are checked at the survey.

  • Battery safety

    LiFePO4 with a battery management system and a fuse right at the battery.

  • Maintenance

    Clean the solar panel every 1 to 3 months, check the tubing, run periodic calibration checks and watch the battery remotely.

Sizing the solar supply

Illustrative example – actual sizing is calculated for each farm.
ItemExample A: 4 silos, 4GExample B: 1–2 silos, LoRa
Assumed average loadabout 6.5 Wabout 3 W
Energy per dayabout 190 Whabout 85 Wh
Battery for 4 days without sunabout 1.05 kWh: LiFePO4 12.8 V 100 Ahabout 470 Wh: LiFePO4 12.8 V 50 Ah
Solar panelabout 170 W, rounded up to 200 Wabout 75 W, rounded up to 100 W
Charge controllerMPPT 12 V 20 AMPPT 10 A

These are engineering assumptions, not manufacturer specifications. Method: energy per day = average load × 24 h × 1.2; battery = energy per day × days without sun ÷ 0.8 ÷ 0.9; solar panel = energy per day ÷ (sun hours in the worst month × 0.75) × 2. Here 1.2 is a 20% margin; 0.8 is the maximum depth of discharge of the battery; 0.9 is the charge and discharge efficiency; 0.75 covers real losses at the solar panel (heat, dust, wiring, charge controller); the factor 2 lets the panel recharge the battery after days without sun. Assumed 4 days without sun and 3 sun hours a day in the worst month. In northern Vietnam in winter the solar panel and battery may need to be larger; HearS sizes them from solar irradiation data for the farm's location at the survey. The power draw of the chosen indicator, totalizer and modem is confirmed at survey and in the quotation.

Confirmed at survey and in the quotation

  • Type of totalizer (mechanical or electronic) and indicator
  • Actual power draw of every device in the cabinet
  • LoRa range on site, or 4G coverage
  • Battery charging limits in hot weather
  • Protective coating for electronics near ammonia
  • Dust zone rules near fill points
  • Silo legs, capacity and number of load cells
  • Expected battery service life

The quotation states the scope clearly: equipment, installation, commissioning, remote viewing of the readings and warranty terms.
