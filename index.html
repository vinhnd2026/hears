Core technology: Hydrostatic™ weighing

Each load cell turns the load into fluid pressure. Tubing carries it to a Summing Totalizer in a clean room, where it becomes one electronic signal. Water, washdown and lightning at the scale do not reach any electronic part there.

“Never Replace Your Load Cells Again!”

That is Emery Winslow Scale’s own line for its Hydrostatic™ load cells — backed by a lifetime guarantee against water and lightning damage. It is the technology HearS is built around.

Built for wet, corrosive and hygienic areas

Hydrostatic load cells carry weight as fluid pressure through tubing to a totalizer in a clean area, so there are no electronics at the weighing site.

  • Food and beverage plants with frequent washdown
  • Chemical plants with corrosive environments
  • Tanks where the weighing point is hard to reach
