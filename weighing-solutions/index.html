Weighing in harsh conditions

Weighing a tank sounds simple until the plant is wet, hot or corrosive. Electronic load cells need cables and protection in exactly the places that are washed down, cleaned with chemicals or hard to reach. Hydrostatic weighing moves the electronics away from the vessel.

Typical situations

  • Daily washdown of tanks and floors
  • Corrosive atmospheres and cleaning agents
  • Vessels in tight or hard-to-reach positions
  • Existing vessels that need weighing added

    Consult

  4. 04

    Commission and support

    We support calibration, start-up and questions afterwards.

What to tell us

  • The vessel type, its contents and the process
  • Capacity range and the resolution you need
  • How the vessel is supported today
  • The cleaning method and chemicals used
  • Surrounding area requirements, including any hazardous-area rules
  • The signal, display and reporting you need
